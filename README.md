# ✅ College Event Feedback Analysis 

**Data Science & Analytics Task 3 – Future Interns**
---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **student feedback** from a college event conducted as part of the **Data Science & Analytics Internship**.
The goal is to **evaluate event quality**, **understand student satisfaction trends**, and **derive actionable recommendations** for future events.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand **average student ratings** across various aspects of the event.
* Perform **sentiment analysis** on textual feedback to gauge overall opinion.
* Identify **correlations between rating criteria** to see which aspects influence satisfaction most.
* Provide **data-driven recommendations** for event improvements.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python** – Data processing and analysis
* **Data Visualization** – Charts and heatmaps
* **Sentiment Analysis** – Measuring positivity and negativity in feedback
* **WordClouds** – Highlight frequent themes in comments

---

## 📁 Dataset

* **Source:** College event feedback survey
* **Format:** CSV
* **Content:** Student ratings for multiple aspects of the event and optional textual feedback

---

## 📊 Analysis Summary

**Rating Analysis:**

* Students provided ratings for aspects like teaching quality, use of presentations, assignment difficulty, doubt-solving, course structure, and recommendation likelihood.
* Analysis highlighted the **strongest and weakest areas** of the event according to student feedback.

**Sentiment Analysis:**

* Textual feedback was categorized as **Positive, Negative, or Neutral**.
* Overall sentiment gave a clear picture of student satisfaction and areas for improvement.

**Correlation Analysis:**

* Relationships between different rating criteria were studied.
* This helped identify which aspects most strongly influenced overall satisfaction.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Highest Rated Aspect:** Knowledge and clarity of the subject delivery
* **Lowest Rated Aspect:** Course structure and difficulty alignment
* Students appreciated clear teaching and subject mastery.
* Areas like engagement, structure, and difficulty alignment need enhancement.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

* Improve **lower-rated aspects** such as course structure and difficulty alignment.
* Encourage **interactive sessions** for better engagement.
* Provide **additional learning resources** for advanced students.
* Address **recurring issues** highlighted in negative feedback comments.

---

## 📌 Visualization Examples

* **Bar Charts:** Show average ratings across different criteria.
* **Heatmaps:** Display correlations between rating aspects.
* **WordClouds:** Highlight frequent words in student feedback.
* **Sentiment Distribution:** Visualize positive, neutral, and negative feedback proportion.

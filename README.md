# ✅ College Event Feedback Analysis

**Data Science & Analytics Task 3 – Future Interns**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **student feedback** from a college event conducted as part of the **Data Science & Analytics Internship**.
The goal was to **evaluate event quality**, **understand student satisfaction trends**, and **derive actionable recommendations** for improving future events.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand **average student ratings** across various aspects of the event
* Perform **sentiment analysis** on textual feedback to gauge overall opinion
* Identify **correlations between rating criteria** to see which aspects influence satisfaction most
* Provide **data-driven recommendations** for enhancing future events

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python** – Data processing, cleaning, and analysis
* **Data Visualization** – Bar charts, heatmaps, and WordClouds
* **Sentiment Analysis** – Categorizing feedback into Positive, Negative, or Neutral
* **WordClouds** – Highlight recurring themes and keywords in textual feedback

---

## 📁 Dataset

* **Source:** [Student Feedback Survey Responses – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ruchi798/student-feedback-survey-responses)
* **Format:** CSV
* **Content:** Student ratings for multiple aspects of the event, along with optional textual feedback

---

## 📊 Analysis Summary

### **Rating Analysis**

* Students rated aspects like teaching quality, use of presentations, assignment difficulty, doubt-solving, course structure, and recommendation likelihood.
* Highlighted **strongest and weakest areas** based on student perception.

### **Sentiment Analysis**

* Feedback was categorized as **Positive, Neutral, or Negative**.
* Provided a **clear picture of overall satisfaction** and areas needing improvement.

### **Correlation Analysis**

* Studied relationships between different rating criteria.
* Identified which aspects most strongly influenced overall satisfaction.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Highest Rated Aspect:** Knowledge and clarity of subject delivery
* **Lowest Rated Aspect:** Course structure and difficulty alignment
* Students appreciated **clarity in teaching and subject mastery**
* Aspects like **engagement, course structure, and difficulty alignment** need improvement

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

* Improve **lower-rated aspects** such as course structure and difficulty alignment
* Incorporate **interactive sessions** to boost engagement
* Provide **additional resources** for advanced learners
* Address **recurring issues** highlighted in negative feedback comments

---

## 📌 Visualization Examples

* **Bar Charts:** Average ratings across criteria
* **Heatmaps:** Correlation between different rating aspects
* **WordClouds:** Frequent themes in textual feedback
* **Sentiment Distribution:** Proportion of positive, neutral, and negative feedback
